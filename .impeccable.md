## Design Context

### Users
The owner and other users of a self-hosted Vectr instance capture screens, annotate them, record short clips, and share images/files from a Windows tray utility. Frequent jobs are finding a recent capture, copying its link, checking upload failures, opening its local file, and pinning a reference.

### Brand Personality
Practical, precise, unobtrusive. Help users act confidently with clear state, predictable shortcuts, and explicit local-versus-remote actions.

### Aesthetic Direction
The owner selected a compact dark desktop utility for the unified History concept on 2026-11-14. ShareX is the interaction reference, with its duplicate History/Image history windows explicitly rejected. Prefer dense but readable native-app controls, restrained accents, clear typography, and image-first browsing without decorative dashboard widgets.

### Design Principles
1. One dataset, one window, alternate views: keep filters, selection, and actions consistent.
2. Make common actions fast through visible controls, context menus, and keyboard access.
3. Show upload status and local-file availability separately; never imply a missing file means a broken remote link.
4. Preserve preview space while letting users collapse detail panes and adjust density.
5. Keep consequential actions explicit, accessible, and distinguishable by text as well as color.
